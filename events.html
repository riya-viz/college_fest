Event Details

1. Code Sprint 24

A 24 hour hackathon for teams of 2 to 4 students. Problem statements are given at 9:00 am on day one and are based on civic problems, campus automation and accessibility. Teams build a working prototype in the lab and then demo it for 7 minutes to the judges. Internet and power backup are provided, laptops you bring. Prize money is Rs 40,000.

2. Battle of Bands

Bands of maximum 6 members get a 15 minute slot on the amphitheatre stage, setup time included. At least one song must be an original composition. Drum kit, amplifiers and sound system are arranged by us. Bands bring their own guitars, sticks and cymbals.

3. Nritya Dhara

Classical and folk dance competition for solo dancers and groups up to 12 members. Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Odissi, Bhangra and Garba are all allowed. Performance should be 6 to 8 minutes and the music file must reach the coordinator two days earlier.

4. RoboRace Arena

Teams race their own wired or wireless bots on a track with ramps, a seesaw and a gravel patch. Bot size limit is 30 x 30 x 30 cm and weight limit is 5 kg. Each team gets 2 practice runs and 2 timed runs, and the better timing is counted.

5. Nukkad Natak

Street play in the Central Courtyard with no microphones and no extra props. Each team gets 12 minutes. The social theme is announced one week before the festival. Script, voice and audience response are judged.

6. Shutterbug Hunt

A full day photography hunt. Five themes are announced at 8:00 am and entries close at 4:00 pm. Submit one photo per theme, clicked on the same day, basic editing only. Mobile and DSLR entries are judged separately.
