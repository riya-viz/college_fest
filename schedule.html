Points to Remember

Entry

Bring your college ID card and the registration confirmation, printed or on your phone. Gates open at 8:00 am and close at 10:30 pm.

Clashing events

Nritya Dhara and RoboRace Arena are on the same day. If you registered for both, inform the help desk before 10:00 am so your RoboRace run can be shifted to the later heat.
