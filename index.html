Welcome to Utsaah

Utsaah is the annual festival of Quad AI School of Technology, Patna. Utsaah means enthusiasm in Hindi and that is what these three days are about. Students from nearby colleges come to our campus every February for coding, music, dance, robotics and theatre competitions.

This year we are keeping six main competitions so that every participant gets enough stage time. Along with the competitions there are food stalls, a gaming zone and an open art wall for visitors.

Festival details

  • Dates: 12, 13 and 14 February 2027 (Friday to Sunday)
  • Venue: Main Campus Grounds, Bailey Road, Patna
  • Timing: 8:00 am to 10:00 pm daily
  • Fee: starts from Rs 100 per event, combo pass Rs 650

Day 1 - Friday
